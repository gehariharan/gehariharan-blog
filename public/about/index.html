Who I Am

I’m Hariharan (known online as @gehariharan). I lead product and engineering efforts focusing on enterprise AI, cloud systems, and developer platforms.

My background bridges software engineering and business leadership (MBA from XLRI, Jamshedpur). Over the course of my career, I’ve led engineering and product architecture initiatives across enterprise software, large-scale cloud infrastructure, and AI-native developer workflows.

For a complete view of my professional background, career trajectory, and leadership roles, visit my LinkedIn profile.

What I Focus On & Build

My guiding philosophy as a builder is grounded in systems thinking: deeply understanding core customer friction, cutting through organizational complexity, and architecting simple, reliable, and high-leverage products.

  • Enterprise AI & Agentic Architectures: Designing multi-step agent pipelines, custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool layers, streaming interactions, and human-in-the-loop interfaces where critical decisions remain observable and controllable.
  • High-Throughput & Operational Systems: Architecting real-time platforms for high-velocity workflows — from live pricing and trading engines to enterprise procurement pipelines and distributed operations.
  • Production Engineering & Systems Discipline: Grounding modern software in the fundamentals that ensure long-term resilience: clean architecture, robust testing, performance, security, and maintainable interfaces.

Endurance Cycling & Pacing

Outside of technology, long-distance cycling across the Pacific Northwest is my lifelong discipline and mental reset. Riding mountain passes teaches pacing, patience, and how to stay calm when the climb gets steep:

  • RAMROD (Ride Around Mount Rainier in One Day): 150+ miles with 10,000+ ft of elevation gain circling Mt. Rainier.
  • Iconic Cascades Climbs: Mt. Baker Artist Point, Mt. Rainier Sunrise Point, North Cascades Highway, and the 200-mile Seattle-to-Portland (STP) ride.

Community & Social Initiatives

I believe in taking direct action when community needs arise. In March 2020, during acute pandemic PPE shortages, my wife Sakthi and I organized the Seattle Face Mask Army — coordinating 100+ volunteers across the Puget Sound to cut, sew, and deliver over 5,000 cloth masks to local healthcare workers and frontline staff. Over the years, I’ve also used endurance rides to raise funds supporting rural education and schooling initiatives.
