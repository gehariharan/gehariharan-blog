DeepReflekt

• Pivoted & Shelved
AI Systems · Leadership Reflection

Leadership development platform centered on recurring reflection capture, collaborative coach-leader continuity loops, and AI-assisted thematic insight generation across executive coaching cycles.

The Pivot: While standalone enterprise adoption was slow, the reflection capture models and AI interaction engine were repurposed and directly powered the coaching core of Aaroha Academy.

OroB

• Pivoted
FinTech · Spot Trading & Metals

Real-time bullion trading and dynamic spot pricing platform for institutional dealer networks. Built live market rates ingestion, SKU-level spread calculation engines, multi-tenant pricing controls, and trading desk order workflows.

What I explored: Sub-second streaming market data, physical delivery hedging models, and high-trust transaction workflows in physical commodity markets.

SchemaDesigner

• Shelved Prototype
DeepTech · AI for Semiconductor Design

Exploration of AI-assisted analog chip design and circuit synthesis. Built a functional end-to-end prototype including schematic parsing, SPICE simulation verification loops, and a web-based circuit visualizer.

Why it was shelved: Learned the deep analog design, validation, and verification pipeline and proved feasibility, but the project paused when our domain-expert partner shifted focus to other priorities.

AgriTech Rewards & Loyalty Platform

• Built · Did Not Sustain
AgriTech · Field Logistics

Geo-aware rewards program and distributor loyalty platform for agricultural enterprises. Implemented geo-fenced QR validation, dealer ledger settlement, and instant redemption disbursements for field farmers.

Why it did not sustain: The offline-tolerant mobile flow and anti-fraud location algorithms worked reliably in field tests, but sustained distributor engagement and manual dealer onboarding proved too high friction to maintain.

Seattle Face Mask Army

• Mission Completed
Community · Grassroots Logistics

Rapid-response decentralized supply chain initiative launched during the March 2020 COVID-19 mask shortage. Mobilized 100+ home volunteers to cut, stitch, sanitize, and distribute 5,000+ cloth masks to Puget Sound healthcare workers and frontline public services.

Outcome: Successfully fulfilled urgent hospital and clinic shortages across King & Snohomish counties before commercial PPE manufacturing stabilized.
